The Founder's Journey

Pacific Trails Resort was born out of frustration. Our founder spent years traveling the coast, searching for a destination where he could truly relax. Every resort he visited forced a compromise: either it was too rigid to actually unwind, or it lacked the dynamic outdoor activities he needed to recharge. He wanted a destination that offered both absolute tranquility and exhilarating adventure. Realizing that the ideal getaway didn't exist, he decided to build it himself.

Creating the Perfect Paradise

Nestled along the coastline in Zephyr, California, our mission is to craft the ultimate seaside paradise. We believe that true luxury means never having to choose between rest and exploration. That is why we offer a vibrant landscape of activities designed to let you engage with nature on your own terms. The Pacific Trails Resort isn't just a destination, it is a dedicated team working around the clock to ensure you enjoy a once-in-a-lifetime experience designed to be repeated. No matter your reason for visiting, we guarantee you won't find a better resort for the occasion.

Your Adventure Awaits

We offer all-weather packages year-round. Come witness the brilliant orange and red leaves fall in autumn, or watch the clear, blue water crash against the coastline in the summer. Unwind in your own private luxury yurt, enjoy a fresh, cooked-to-order breakfast, and watch hummingbirds hard at work. After a thrilling day of ocean kayaking or simply resting by the ocean, you'll return to be greeted by the elegant oystercatchers that share our shores. Because no two guests are alike, we ensure that no two adventures are the same. Although we offer plenty of beautiful views, none are more important to us than the smile on your face when you realize you've found your new home away from home!
