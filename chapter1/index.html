My favorite beverage is Sprite. There is nothing that can beat the crispiness of a cold Sprite. The balance of lemon and lime adds a citrus, sour taste that really balances out the large amounts of sugar. My favorite rendition of the sprite is the sprite remix but i dont think they make those anymore. When i was kid they were popular, it was like the fruit punch version. I still prefer the regular version over any other drink in the market especially if its in a can.
